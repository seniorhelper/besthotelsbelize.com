Privacy Policy and Affiliate Disclosure

Updated November 10, 2026

Best Hotels Belize (besthotelsbelize.com) is a free guide to places to stay in Belize, from Ambergris Caye and the cayes to Placencia and the Cayo District. This page covers what data is collected when you use the site, which outside services set cookies, and how the site is paid for.

The short version

  • You don't need an account and we don't sell personal information.
  • Analytics, ads and the Expedia hotel search use cookies.
  • If you book a stay through the hotel search, we may earn a commission.
  • If you send us a message, we use it only to reply.

Information collected automatically

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical details such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability. We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which guides are useful. It uses cookies and similar technology to record things like pages viewed, approximate location (city or region level), device type and how you arrived. We don't use it to identify you personally.

Messages and forms

If you fill in a form, for example to ask about a featured listing, we receive what you type (such as your name, email, phone number and message) along with the page you sent it from. Form messages are delivered through our own lead server at clients.eyetoad.com, run by our publisher, Eye To Ad Media, and are used only to answer you. We don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

Expedia hotel search and affiliate links

Some pages include a hotel search box supplied by Expedia Group through its affiliate program. When you use it or follow it to Expedia, Expedia Group and its affiliate-tracking partners may set or read cookies on your device to record that you came from this site. If you then book, we may receive a commission. You don't pay more. Searches and bookings are handled by Expedia under Expedia's privacy statement, not by us. We don't see your payment details.

Commissions don't decide which hotels or areas we describe. We aren't a travel agent and can't change or cancel bookings.

Advertising

We may show ads served by Google, including Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners serve ads to you based on those visits. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. More on how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Paid placements from businesses are labeled "Featured" or "Sponsored", and links in them are marked as sponsored.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them won't stop you reading the guide, though the hotel search may not work fully. To ask what we hold about you from a form message, or to have it deleted, use the contact page.

Children

The site is for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
