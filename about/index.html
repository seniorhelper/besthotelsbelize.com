About Best Hotels Belize

Updated November 10, 2026

Best Hotels Belize helps travelers choose where to stay in a small country with very different corners: reef-side cayes, the Placencia peninsula, and jungle lodges near the Maya sites of the Cayo District. The aim is to help you pick the right area first, then the right place to stay in it.

Who's behind it

The guide is published by Eye To Ad Media, an independent web publisher and digital marketing company in Denver, Colorado, run by Zach Wennstedt. We publish a small group of travel guides for the Caribbean, Central America and Hawaiʻi.

We don't run a hotel, sell tours or take bookings. Questions about a reservation go to the property or to the site you booked on.

How places get on our pages

  • Only real, operating properties. Every resort and lodge we name can be looked up and booked directly.
  • Area before hotel. We explain what it's like to stay on Ambergris Caye versus a private caye, or near San Ignacio versus deep in the forest, because that choice shapes a Belize trip more than room features do.
  • No invented ratings or reviews. We don't publish star scores or made-up guest quotes. For guest opinions, read verified reviews on booking sites.
  • Prices change, so we rarely print them. Where we mention typical costs (like the flight to San Pedro), treat them as rough guides and check before you go.

How the guide is paid for

Reading is free. Some pages carry an Expedia hotel search; if you book through it, we may earn a commission at no cost to you. We may also show ads, and we sell clearly labeled featured placements to local businesses. None of that buys a place in our hotel lists. Details are in our privacy and affiliate disclosure.

Found a mistake?

Hotels change hands and close for renovations. If something here is out of date, let us know with a link to the property's own website, and we'll check and fix it.
